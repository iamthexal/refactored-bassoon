Normal Characters


Shigekata Odoriba
“Time spent on frivolities is time stolen from those who came before you.”
	  

	Patriarch of the Odoriba family, and the head of the family’s shrine. He is deeply steeped in tradition, which in many ways makes him quite rigid and inflexible, especially in dealing with his children who appear to share little interest in priestly duties. 
He is especially critical of his son, believing that his focus should be on learning how to run the shrine which he will one day inherit rather than on frivolous things. While Kazumasa would never confront him on this, Etsuko does – and the two of them often butt heads and have a strained relationship. 
While he does not wish to hinder his children’s potential, he does want them to lead successful lives and believes that he and his family have already carved out a successful niche for them in the shrine. He would prefer that they focus on this rather than things he considers “frivolous”, and things that he believes could potentially endanger the family’s reputation. 
	

Tetsuo Yamanashi
"Why am I still here? I should've died a long time ago..."
	  

	Utako’s grandfather and Motoshige’s father. Served as a fighter pilot during the Pacific campaign of WWII, and was one of the many soldiers who attacked Pearl Harbor on December 7th, 1941. However, upon being shot down, his plane crashed into the water and he was captured by US forces and held as a prisoner of war until 1955 when he was finally able to return home.
As an old man, he suffers from Alzheimer’s, and as such, it has affected his memory, causing him to drift in and out of the past and present. However, he remains lucid most of the time and is quite talkative, especially about his time serving in the war and his time as a POW. He seems to have mixed feelings about the war - while he is proud to have served his country, he also feels deep regret for the lives he took, and often expresses a desire to have died instead of those he killed.
	

Jota Oomiya
“If it weren’t for you, we could’ve gone to the nationals!”
	  

	A batter belonging to Seisei high’s baseball team, and a third year student. Dreaming of going pro, his ambitions were dashed after Takeru Magohei let his ego get the best of him and made a play which cost them the game.
He hasn’t forgiven him for this and holds onto it as a grudge, along with several other players. He believes he’ll never be scouted again because of this failure on Takeru’s part.
There was a notable change in his demeanor ever since this, as he became something more of a delinquent.
	



Principal Sudo
“It is imperative to be mindful of how time is spent.”
	  

	The principal of Seisei high school, renowned for being incredibly firm and strict. He doesn’t have a tolerance for troublemakers or slackers, and has been known to fly off the rails at times. He is  often mockingly referred to as “Dictator Sudo” by the student body.
He deeply cares about the reputation of Seisei high, somewhat selfishly – as the condition of the school ultimately reflects on him. This has led to him making numerous decisions to protect the school’s reputation at the cost of ease and comfort for the student body. 
It’s also not much of a secret that he is in Eihi’s pocket, as he seems to draw exceptions to rules whenever he is involved. He is a man who has let the illusion of success take up all of his time.

Shadows


Shadow Takeru
“My future died the day I struck out.”
	  

	A Shadow born from the psyche of Takeru Magohei’s belief that he squandered his future and walks an aimless path. 


As such, his Shadow is both deeply nihilistic and egotistical. To him, time and the future only matter when it benefits himself – otherwise, it does not matter to him at all. 


He is incredibly arrogant as well, and cannot stand to be at a disadvantage or to lose.
	

Shadow Saburo
“You love me, don't you? I know you love me. You're mine, Yukina. You're all I've got.”
	  

	After the divorce, Yukina’s father, Saburo was so distraught that he believed that Yukina was the only thing he had left to love in the world.

A businessman and CEO of a massive conglomerate. While on the outside he seems like a generous philanthropist who only has the interests of the public at heart, in truth, he is a sociopath who views human life as nothing more than a commodity.
His company is called "Tokyo Eikou Co." and has investments in various industries - everything from healthcare and pharmaceuticals to tech and telecommunications. He also owns a number of private military contractors, which he uses to further his own ends.

Eihi
“The only good thing about this backwater place is the view... and even that’s barely tolerable..”
	  

	The son of Chuuzenji, and the prince of his fortune. He attends Seisei High as a third year student, and ever since enrolling, the school has more or less been in his father’s pocket. He’s allowed to come and go as he pleases, often skipping school and facing no penalties. He can get away with just about anything, since if anyone tries to challenge him – all it takes is one phone call. 
He has personally financed and arranged numerous events to the point that the school debates the purpose of the student council in the future, which has placed him silently at odds with its members. These events, though, usually only serve to give him a spotlight above anyone else. He has a bit of a narcissistic personality, and doesn’t care to have friends nor connections – as loyalty, to him, is always bought.  
He is in on his father’s desire to understand Chronos and the Aion, serving as his eyes and ears on a lower level. 
	

Noburu Tameyasu
“It's just business.”
	  

	An infamous Yakuza boss active within Kanatajima, who deals in everything from drug and human trafficking, to even money laundering. It’s rumored that he’s in league with some pretty powerful people who funnel money to him in exchange for taking care of things like whistleblowers or company secrets, and the chief of police is rumored to be in his pocket – hence how he’s able to get away with all of this. 
He prefers to prey primarily on high school students, notably ones who struggle with self-image, with his soldiers often forcing them into debts they cannot possibly repay. 
He lives a luxurious life built off of the desperation of others, and to him, time is nothing. He has all the time he could ever want.
