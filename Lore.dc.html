AION
THE COLLECTIVE UNCONSCIOUS PERCEPTION OF TIME
The supernatural realm of this setting is known as the Aion, which represents the collective unconscious perception of time. It presents itself almost as a mirror of the real world, however, the flow of time in this realm is not linear, and it looks as though various different eras of the past, present, and future have been mashed together, inhabited by the archetypal (generic) Shadows of the human consciousness.
!
It is possible to tamper with the collective perception of time in the real world within Aion, but doing so would require the influence of powerful forces…

Mirages are delusions which manifest as distorted demiplanes representative of the darker side of a person’s psyche in reference to a strained and harmful relationship with time as a concept, be it fighting against it or refusing to let go of it.

As part of Chronos, time flows more linearly within a Mirage, and they are typically shaped by things such as memories, long-lost goals, etc.

KANATAJIMA
THE ISLAND
LONG AGO
1970s
20XX
A long time ago, Kanatajima was a small volcanic island within the pacific, only a bit off the coast of Tokyo. The communities there were largely small, rural fishing villages,
up until the 1970s where tourism began to explode.
The cities and towns began to rapidly expand to make up for this sudden boom in tourism and residency.
